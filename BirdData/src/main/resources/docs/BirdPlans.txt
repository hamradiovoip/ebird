GET Recent observations in a region   https://api.ebird.org/v2/data/obs/{{regionCode}}/recent

GET Recent notable observations in a region : https://api.ebird.org/v2/data/obs/{{regionCode}}/recent/notable

GET Recent observations of a species in a region : https://api.ebird.org/v2/data/obs/{{regionCode}}/recent/{{speciesCode}}

GET Recent nearby observations : https://api.ebird.org/v2/data/obs/geo/recent?lat={{lat}}&lng={{lng}}

GET Recent nearby observations of a species : https://api.ebird.org/v2/data/obs/geo/recent/{{speciesCode}}?lat={{lat}}&lng={{lng}}

GET Nearest observations of a species : https://api.ebird.org/v2/data/nearest/geo/recent/{{speciesCode}}?lat={{lat}}&lng={{lng}}

GET Recent nearby notable observations   :  https://api.ebird.org/v2/data/obs/geo/recent/notable?lat={{lat}}&lng={{lng}}

GET Historic observations on a date : https://api.ebird.org/v2/data/obs/{{regionCode}}/historic/{{y}}/{{m}}/{{d}}
